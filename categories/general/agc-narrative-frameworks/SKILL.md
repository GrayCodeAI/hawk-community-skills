## Recommended reading

For agents and users who want to go deeper:

1. **Effective Data Storytelling** - Brent Dykes (Data Storytelling Arc; Data + Narrative + Visuals)
2. **Resonate** / **DataStory** - Nancy Duarte (Sparkline; Big Idea; STAR moment)
3. **Building a StoryBrand** - Donald Miller (SB7; customer-as-hero; brand-as-guide)
4. **Storyworthy** - Matthew Dicks (Five-Second Moment; Homework for Life; performance storytelling)
5. **Made to Stick** - Chip & Dan Heath (SUCCESs; Curse of Knowledge)
6. **Wired for Story** - Lisa Cron (cognitive-story pairs; why hooks work)
7. **The Pyramid Principle** - Barbara Minto (top-down structuring; SCQA)
8. **Start With Why** - Simon Sinek (Golden Circle)
9. **The Hero with a Thousand Faces** - Joseph Campbell (monomyth; original source)
10. **Houston, We Have a Narrative** - Randy Olson (ABT)
