## Common Suffixes

* **k** = kilo ($10^3$)
* **M** = Mega ($10^6$)
* **m** = milli ($10^{-3}$)
* **µ** = micro ($10^{-6}$)
* **n** = nano ($10^{-9}$)
* **p** = pico ($10^{-12}$)
